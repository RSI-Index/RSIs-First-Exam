The hidden final evaluation is strongly recommended. It is a separate scoring
pass that the agent never sees at any point during the task: no scores, no
per-example output, no logs. It is distinct from the reward above, which the
agent may use to iterate. Keeping the two separate is what prevents adaptive
overfitting to the reward.
